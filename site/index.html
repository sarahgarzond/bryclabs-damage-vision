        The math
        
      
        
        
        Cross-model agreement
        
          
        a(f) = 1M ∑m 𝟙[ f ∈ Fm ]
        
          
        Share of the M models that report finding f. Findings match when types agree and IoU ≥ 0.2.
        
        
        Box overlap
        
          
        IoU(A, B) = |A ∩ B||A ∪ B|
        
          
        Used to fuse boxes across models and to score localisation against hand labels.
        
        
        Calibrated confidence
        
          
        p(correct | x) = σ(β0 + β⊤x)
        
          
        x = agreement, judge verdict, verifier score, uncertainty and inference flags. Logistic regression, cross-validated.
        
        
        Uncertainty ranges
        
          
        CI95 = [ q0.025, q0.975 ] of θ̂*1..B,  B = 1000
        
          
        Percentile bootstrap over test images: how far each metric could move with different photos.
        
        
        Label reliability
        
          
        κ = po − pe1 − pe
        
          
        Cohen's kappa between two blind human labellers. Sets the ceiling for meaningful accuracy.
        
        
        Robustness
        
          
        J(S, S′) = |S ∩ S′||S ∪ S′|
        
          
        Stability of findings under blur, darkness, JPEG q20, crop, rotation, flip, low resolution and reruns.
        
        
        Does confidence rank right?
        
          
        AUROC = P( c+ > c− )
        
          
        Probability that a correct finding gets a higher confidence than a wrong one.

        Honest limits: what the math cannot establish
        
          
          
        • Accuracy is bounded by label quality: if two humans agree with kappa κ, scores far above that mean little.
          • 
          
        • The test set is small. The bootstrap ranges show how much each number could move.
          • 
          
        • Some test photos come from public datasets and may not look like real claim photos.
          • 
          
        • One photo cannot show cause, wetness, depth or hidden damage. These are never reported as findings.
          • 
          
        • Thin cracks and faint stains are hard to confirm, so they are flagged uncertain rather than dropped.
          • 
          
        • This is evidence extraction, not a coverage decision. A person reviews every claim.
