    Known limits
    
        
      
          
    • Accuracy is only as good as our labels: two humans agree on damage types with the kappa shown above, so higher scores than that mean little.
      • 
          
    • The test set is small. The ranges show how much each number could move with a different sample of photos.
      • 
          
    • Our test photos partly come from public datasets and may not look like real claim photos.
      • 
          
    • One photo cannot show cause, wetness, depth or hidden damage. These are never reported as findings.
      • 
          
    • Thin cracks and faint stains are often missed by the detector, so they are flagged uncertain rather than confirmed.
      • 
          
    • Confidence scores are only meaningful once calibrated on labelled photos. Each result says whether that has happened.
      • 
          
    • This is evidence extraction, not a coverage decision. A person reviews every claim.
