How it works

          
Every finding has to survive two independent checks before we call it established.

        

        
    
          
  1. 1
    Extract
    A vision-language model (Qwen-VL) reads the photo and must answer in our JSON schema with a fixed vocabulary.
    2. 
          
  2. 2
    Repeat
    It runs several times on slightly varied copies. A finding seen in every run gets high agreement.
    3. 
          
  3. 3
    Verify
    An independent detector (OWLv2) looks for each finding at its claimed position.
    4. 
          
  4. 4
    Score
    Unsupported findings are dropped, unconfirmed ones flagged uncertain. Confidence is calibrated on our labels.
    5. 
          
  5. 5
    Summarise
    Claim readiness: what is established, what is only indicated, and which photos to request next.
    6.

What a single photo cannot tell

            
    
              
  • The cause of damage, only visible indicators of it
    • 
              
  • Whether a stain is still wet, or how deep a crack goes
    • 
              
  • Anything outside the frame or behind surfaces
    • 
              
  • Whether the claim is covered: that is the policy check and human review
